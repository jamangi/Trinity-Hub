# BO-05 — Isolation Notebook

Status: proposed; a blank reasoning worksheet can begin now. Initial user: green jacket.

## Purpose

Make your reasoning stronger before a long retest or a parts request. Reduce repeated tests and guesses by separating what is known from what still needs investigation.

## Function

A personal case worksheet: symptom, candidate explanation, supporting/contradicting evidence, next distinguishing check, actual result, and what remains unresolved. SIFT finds gaps and summarizes evidence already provided. It does not authorize parts, decide release, or claim a fault is isolated merely because a replacement was followed by success.

## Gather

- A case timeline, actions already performed, exact observations, and their conditions.
- The applicable test/procedure references and the authority needed for candidate next steps.
- What evidence Diagnostic or Repair needs to avoid repeating your investigation. See [Q05, Q06, and Q09](../context/open-questions.md).

## Build sequence and SIFT prompt

1. Copy the [isolation worksheet](../templates/isolation-note.md) for a synthetic practice case or a permitted local work record.
2. Ask SIFT to distinguish facts from hypotheses and identify whether proposed tests actually separate the candidates.
3. Record the actual result before revising the hypothesis. Preserve inconclusive results and changed conditions.
4. After verified resolution, extract a scoped learning entry into the workplace knowledge base. Feed a reviewed summary to the handoff aid.

> Review the opened investigation notes. Separate observations from candidate explanations. For each proposed next check, explain which candidates it could distinguish using only the supplied procedures. Flag repeated checks with unchanged conditions, unsupported parts conclusions, and missing evidence. If no supported next check is available, state the question to escalate instead of inventing a command.

Apply the [common SIFT instruction](README.md).

## Machiavellian Index

- **Pain Addressed: 4/5.** Guesswork can consume parts, suite time, and other teams' attention.
- **Rank Needed: green for your own reasoning.** Parts approval and other teams' diagnostic decisions remain with their existing owners.
- **Obnoxiousness: 1/5.** Share evidence that makes a handoff easier; do not score colleagues or turn the worksheet into a new gate they must satisfy.

## Try, measure, and decide

Use a case with two plausible causes and insufficient evidence: the worksheet must preserve both rather than force a diagnosis. Check summaries against the original timeline. Track avoided repetitions and whether the next team can use your evidence. Reduce the form if it duplicates ServiceMe fields or slows routine work. You own the personal worksheet; broader use depends on voluntary usefulness and a reviewer for published lessons.

## Next dependency

Practice the distinction between evidence and inference now. Any operational test recommendation waits for the applicable source and conditions.
